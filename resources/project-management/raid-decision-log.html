Keep risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies, and decisions together, with explicit ownership and next actions. Filter the register, identify overdue items, and export a record for reporting or handoff.

Entries stay in this browser session until exported. This page does not send project information to a server or AI system. Use synthetic or approved public information in examples.

Working principles

Risk: something uncertain that could affect the work. Record probability, impact, response, and owner.

Assumption: an unverified belief that needs confirmation.

Issue: an event already occurring and requiring a response.

Dependency: work that waits on another team, resource, or approval.

Decision: what was selected, by whom, why, and what changes as a result.

Scores prioritize discussion, not truth. A probability–impact product is ordinal and should not be interpreted as a calculated likelihood or monetary exposure.
