# Curriculum & Lesson Design

Reusable instructional-design resources for moving from learner context and authentic performance to aligned practice, assessment, transfer, and iteration.

## Prepared resource

- [Design Around a Real Decision — Curriculum Planning Canvas](Design_Around_a_Real_Decision_Curriculum_Planning_Canvas.md)

## What it supports

- learner-context and prerequisite analysis;
- authentic outcome definition;
- backward design from real decisions/actions;
- knowledge, evidence, skill, and language mapping;
- scaffolded practice and transfer;
- learner-artifact and feedback planning;
- accessibility and inclusion checks;
- assessment alignment;
- curriculum sequencing;
- update/maintenance planning;
- instructor handoff and evidence-informed revision.

## Related tool

Use the [Curriculum / Workshop QA & Outline Builder](../curriculum-workshop-qa/) after the learning architecture is defined to operationalize timing, activities, assessments, materials, and delivery QA.

The resources are newly authored public derivatives. They do not reproduce institutional curricula or third-party teaching materials.
