Syllabus & Assessment Alignment Builder

Plan a course around what learners should be able to do. Link every objective to instructional practice and evidence of learning; then review grading, workload, resources, and the syllabus information students need.

This local-first planning tool has no account, analytics, or AI connection. Entries remain in the active browser session unless you download them. Avoid entering protected student data. Institutional policies and disciplinary requirements must be checked independently.

6. Alignment review and exports

Review flags

The checks detect missing mappings, incomplete assessment evidence, grading-weight arithmetic, and entered workload thresholds. They cannot judge disciplinary accuracy or certify accessibility.

Export and import

Import replaces the working plan after confirmation. The source file is read locally, with a 400 KB size limit. Export a backup first.
