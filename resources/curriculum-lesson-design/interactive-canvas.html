Method and sources
The canvas draws on authentic performance, backward design, assessment alignment, and the iterative planning work documented in the original curriculum canvas. The objectives section uses an observable action, a knowledge dimension, and revision checks, informed by the Columbia learning-objective exercise undertaken during advanced teaching-and-learning work. It is also consistent with CMU's alignment guidance.
