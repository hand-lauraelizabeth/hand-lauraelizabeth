# Literature Review & Evidence Synthesis Matrix

A research workbook for turning a body of sources into a defensible synthesis rather than a sequence of annotations.

## Prepared artifact

[Download the Excel workbook](Literature_Review_Evidence_Synthesis_Matrix.xlsx)

## Core model

**source → finding → theme → pattern → contradiction → method/context explanation → gap → qualified claim**

## What it supports

- source and finding logs;
- source-by-theme coverage;
- claim testing;
- methodological-composition review;
- contradiction and gap tracking;
- synthesis planning;
- draft support and evidence traceability.

The structure keeps claims connected to evidence while making disagreement, context, and methodological differences visible.
