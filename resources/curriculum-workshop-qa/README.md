# Curriculum / Workshop QA & Outline Builder

A learning-design workbook for developing, reviewing, and handing off workshops with stronger alignment between objectives, activities, timing, assessment, and participant materials.

## Prepared artifact

[Download the Excel workbook](Curriculum_Workshop_QA_and_Outline_Builder.xlsx)

## What it supports

- timed agenda development;
- objective-to-activity mapping;
- participant practice planning;
- assessment coverage;
- materials and facilitator requirements;
- accessibility prompts;
- publication and delivery QA;
- clean handoff between curriculum, faculty, and operations.

The workbook is designed to surface gaps before delivery without turning instructional-design judgment into an opaque score.
