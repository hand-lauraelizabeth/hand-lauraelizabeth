# Research Project Ethics & IRB Readiness Toolkit

A planning workbook for documenting the methods, participant/data, consent, privacy, recruitment, and review questions that should be resolved before data collection.

## Prepared artifact

[Download the Excel workbook](Research_Project_Ethics_and_IRB_Readiness_Toolkit.xlsx)

## What it supports

- research-question, evidence, analysis, and limitation mapping;
- participant and data-flow mapping;
- human-participant and identifiable/private-data prompts;
- sensitive-topic, vulnerable-population, authority-relationship, deception, and consent review;
- privacy and data-minimization planning;
- recruitment and justice checks;
- review-packet tracking and decision logging;
- executive readiness summary.

This is **not an IRB determination engine**. It is designed to help researchers surface questions early and identify when institutional research-ethics or IRB consultation is appropriate.
