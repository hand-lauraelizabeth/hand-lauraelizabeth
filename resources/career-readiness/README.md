# Career Readiness Resource System

A companion resource system for the career-readiness and curriculum-design work in my portfolio.

The system includes two complementary kinds of material:

1. **Applied institutional work** — the Hostos Grow with Google Career Readiness Guide, displayed with permission as an example of my curriculum and resource-design work.
2. **Reusable resources** — tools developed from recurring methods in my career-services, teaching, advising, and curriculum work.

## Resource architecture

### Interview preparation

The interview system treats preparation as an evidence problem rather than a performance script.

**Workflow:**

role requirements → evidence map → story bank → answer planning → interviewer questions → interview-day checks → debrief → reusable evidence record

Core design principles:

- map each important role requirement to specific evidence;
- build stories from real examples rather than memorized scripts;
- separate situation, action, result, and reflection so the user's contribution is clear;
- prepare for questions about prioritization, conflict, feedback, mistakes, ambiguity, leadership, and learning;
- make employer research useful by converting it into better questions;
- treat compensation, schedule, benefits, accessibility, and working conditions as legitimate candidate considerations;
- avoid narrow professionalism rules based on eye contact, handshakes, stillness, vocal patterns, clothing, grooming, or other culturally and disability-sensitive behaviors;
- use the post-interview debrief to improve the evidence bank for the next conversation.

### Career accomplishment inventory

A companion evidence-recovery system helps users identify work they may overlook because it was routine to them: improvements, scale, quality, efficiency, money/resources, growth, systems built, problems solved, leadership, recognition, access/equity, and learning.

The same evidence can then be translated for a résumé, interview, portfolio, performance review, or cover letter without inventing new accomplishments for each format.

## Applied example: Hostos Grow with Google Career Readiness Guide

The portfolio site includes the fillable Hostos Grow with Google Career Readiness Guide as an applied example with permission. My 2023 work on the guide included updating and organizing its career-readiness content for student use. The artifact coordinates career assessment, résumé and cover-letter development, LinkedIn, interviewing, reflection, and reusable student activities.

Because the guide incorporates institutional and third-party content, the website links to the authorized display copy while this repository focuses on the reusable design system and companion resources.

## Attribution & privacy

Resources in this directory are designed for reuse without student records, employer-confidential data, member data, or proprietary institutional datasets. Applied institutional examples are identified separately, and third-party material retains its original attribution and rights.
