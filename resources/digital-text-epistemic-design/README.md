# Digital Text & Epistemic Design

This collection connects Laura Elizabeth Hand's 2019 collaborative epistemic-design work around the Making and Knowing Project's Digital Critical Edition with a reusable 2026 design canvas.

- [Digital Text & Epistemic Design Canvas](Digital_Text_and_Epistemic_Design_Canvas.md)
- [Making and Knowing Project Case Study](Making_and_Knowing_Project_Case_Study.md)

The case study documents the 2019 research and design work; the canvas extends those methods to other digital editions, archives, learning experiences, and evidence-rich interfaces.
