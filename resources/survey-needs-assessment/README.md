# Survey / Needs-Assessment Builder & Analysis Workbook

A survey-research workbook for moving from instrument design through fielding, data-quality review, descriptive analysis, segmentation, and qualified findings.

## Prepared artifact

`Survey_Needs_Assessment_Builder_and_Analysis_Workbook.xlsx`

## What it supports

- item and scale planning;
- fielding documentation;
- consent, completion, attention, straightlining, and missing-data checks;
- item-level descriptive summaries;
- favorable-rate analysis;
- segment comparisons with low-N warnings;
- needs-priority gap analysis;
- open-text coding;
- findings and limitations kept together.

Automated checks flag issues for review; they do not substitute for methodological judgment.
