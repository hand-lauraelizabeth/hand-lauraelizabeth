# Public Tools & Templates — Release Notes

## v1.0.0 — October 1, 2026

First complete public release of the reusable Tools & Templates library.

### Included

- Automated Project Management Control Center
- APA 7 Student Research Paper Template
- APA 7 Research Writing Toolkit Guide
- Research Writing Planner & Evidence Matrix
- Literature Review & Evidence Synthesis Matrix
- Curriculum / Workshop QA & Outline Builder
- Decision, Prioritization & Needs-Assessment System
- Survey / Needs-Assessment Builder & Analysis Workbook
- Research Project Ethics & IRB Readiness Toolkit
- Quantitative Research & Statistical Analysis Planner

### Release standard

The public artifacts use generic, synthetic, public-domain, openly licensed, or otherwise publication-safe examples rather than confidential organizational, learner, client, member, or proprietary source data.

The repository now validates local documentation links and inspects the Office packages for structural integrity, macro/embedded executable-style parts, Excel external-link packages, and local-file relationship targets on relevant pushes and pull requests.

### Distribution

The current files are available from the [Tools & Templates index](README.md) and from the corresponding resource pages on [lauraelizabethhand.com](https://www.lauraelizabethhand.com/resources/).

### Versioning

This release is recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json), which lists each published artifact and its Git blob SHA so file changes can be tracked independently of surrounding documentation.
