CSV layouts and example columns

Wide format: learner_id,assessment_date,Fractions,Argument writing. Each standard has its own column; a blank score means no evidence. Repeating a learner with a different date enables progress comparison.

Long format: learner_id,standard,score,max_score,assessment_date. The last two columns are optional. Student ID aliases: student_id, student, learner, or name. Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD. Only valid scores from 0 through their maximum are analyzed.

Take the plan into your workflow

Export both an instructor plan (including editable notes) and a learner-standard follow-up roster. Spreadsheet-safe CSV cells prevent formula interpretation of learner-supplied text.

Scores are normalized as score ÷ possible points × 100. Score changes are descriptive and do not establish the effect of instruction. This tool cannot determine assessment validity, learning disabilities, accommodations, or the cause of an error.
