Why this structure matters

A polished answer can still be poorly grounded. This workflow makes the relationship between claim and evidence visible, identifies what a source does not establish, and revises language to match the strength of support.

Instructional extension

Ask learners to run the same claim through two versions: one with no evidence constraints and one generated here. Compare unsupported specificity, citation behavior, uncertainty language, and what the model treats as fact. The learning objective is not “write a better prompt”; it is to make the evidence contract visible.

Scope: This demonstration composes prompts; it does not send content to an AI model and does not determine whether a source is authoritative.
