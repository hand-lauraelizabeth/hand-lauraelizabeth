Context Before Count

Objective: Interpret keyword-in-context (KWIC) evidence before making a claim about a corpus. This browser-local exercise adapts the KWIC step of the Python Corpus Explorer teaching artifact. It does not run spaCy, TF-IDF, or named-entity recognition.

All three passages below are invented for instruction. Select a term and examine each occurrence with surrounding context.

Which conclusion is best supported by the displayed evidence?

Applied capstone connection

Bring three short, rights-cleared passages to a corpus analysis. Record each source ID, the search term, its immediate context, and at least one competing interpretation. Compare your interpretation with a simple count; explain what the count cannot establish. Do not submit personal or confidential documents.

Assessment criteria

Successful work identifies at least two distinct uses of the term, quotes the relevant surrounding words, preserves source IDs, and qualifies claims that the corpus cannot support. The multiple-choice check assesses only the initial interpretation; the capstone requires written evidence.
