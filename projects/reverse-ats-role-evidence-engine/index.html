How v2.1 classifies text

This first implementation is deliberately heuristic and inspectable. It splits the posting into sentences/bullets, classifies each line from visible wording, extracts meaningful terms, and searches candidate evidence for exact or normalized phrase support. It does not infer credentials, direct-report relationships, fiscal authority, or proficiency from ambiguous language.
