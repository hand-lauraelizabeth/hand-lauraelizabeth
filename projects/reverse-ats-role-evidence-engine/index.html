How v2.2c classifies text and evidence

The posting parser and evidence graph are deliberately heuristic and inspectable. Candidate evidence is decomposed into source-specific statement nodes with detected scope, dates, metrics, and depth signals; requirement matches point to those nodes. v2.2c adds human-reviewed metadata and claim-strength guardrails. Detection does not establish truth, proficiency, direct-report relationships, fiscal authority, causality, or recency; stronger wording should depend on reviewed evidence, not keyword presence.
