How matching and regional wages work

Occupation leads are ranked using résumé terms that overlap with O*NET occupation titles, alternate titles, descriptions, and core tasks. A target role can refine the ranking. Matches are leads to investigate and do not establish eligibility, fit for a specific opening, or employer interest.

Pay is the middle half of published annual wages (25th to 75th percentile) and the median for a matching BLS occupation in the selected metro or nonmetro area. It describes an occupation across employers at the time of the survey. No offer, current vacancy, or personalized salary prediction is implied. Some combinations lack numeric annual percentiles. A ZIP is matched to its 2020 Census ZIP Code Tabulation Area and then to counties and BLS labor markets. PO boxes, newly assigned ZIPs, or some areas where boundaries changed may be unavailable.

The résumé, posting, and ZIP are processed in this tab. This feature fetches public files. A wage-file request identifies a group of labor-market areas to the file host.
