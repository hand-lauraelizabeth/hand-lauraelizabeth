How to teach with it

Ask learners to diagnose the same vague prompt individually, then compare their ratings. The disagreement is useful: it exposes which assumptions were implicit. Next, rebuild the prompt and test whether the revision changes the AI output in ways that matter—accuracy, traceability, uncertainty, privacy, or usefulness.

Do not grade prompt length. A short prompt can be well specified. A long prompt can still omit the evidence boundary, verification plan, or human decision rule.

Seven dimensions

Task — what must be done; context — audience and purpose; evidence — what may support factual claims; boundaries — what the system must not infer or decide; output — what a usable response looks like; verification — how important claims will be checked; human responsibility — who retains judgment, especially when stakes are high.

Scope: This tool does not evaluate an AI model, determine whether a prompt is “safe,” or provide legal/compliance advice. It is a structured teaching aid for making hidden task assumptions visible.
