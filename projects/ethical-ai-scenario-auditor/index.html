How to teach with it

Have small groups audit the same scenario from different roles: learner or employee, program owner, data steward, security lead, and executive sponsor. Compare which risks become visible from each position. Then revise the scenario—not merely the policy language—so the design itself reduces avoidable risk.

Important: This tool is a structured discussion aid, not a compliance assessment, legal opinion, or substitute for domain-specific review.
