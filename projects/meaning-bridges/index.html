How validation works and what it cannot do

Links are curated for a particular sense, not generated by a live dictionary. The game recognizes multiple paths through the reviewed graph, but it may reject a legitimate synonym not yet included. A shared spelling does not mean every sense is interchangeable. The descriptions explain the intended sense shift. No account, network request, or storage is used.
