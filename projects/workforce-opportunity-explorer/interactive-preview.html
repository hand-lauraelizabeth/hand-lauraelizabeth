How to interpret this explorer

OEWS estimates reflect wage-and-salary employment, not current vacancies, projected openings, the self-employed workforce, or career outcomes. Annual median wage is not a starting salary. Employment counts across overlapping geographies must not be summed. Suppressed values remain unavailable and are excluded from wage rankings.

Occupational groups and SOC codes describe jobs, not degree programs. This preview does not infer educational-program offerings, licensure eligibility, or CIP-to-SOC pathways. ACS PUMS education and earnings analyses require separate geographic and survey-weight checks before integration.
