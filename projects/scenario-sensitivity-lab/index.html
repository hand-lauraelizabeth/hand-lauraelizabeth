How to interpret

The volume effect is (proposed sessions − baseline sessions) × baseline rate. The rate effect is proposed sessions × (proposed rate − baseline rate). These add exactly to the total change. The decomposition depends on the stated order: volume first, rate second. It is not a causal estimate.

For a real forecast, distinguish historical observations from projections; document session types, time periods, fee rules, seasonality and uncertainty. This one-category exercise does not model cancellations, bursts, or negotiated contracts.
