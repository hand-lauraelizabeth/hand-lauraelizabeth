Applied capstone

Before publishing an operational dashboard, write a decision record: (1) what was counted and over what period, (2) what the data do not establish, (3) what comparison or additional evidence would be needed, and (4) whether suppression or aggregation could reveal sensitive information.

Self-check rubric: One point each for unit and period, bounded claim, missing evidence, and disclosure safeguards (0–4). A score of 4 means the record covers all four criteria, not that the underlying conclusion is true.

Teaching prototype · fictional data only · browser-local interactions · no external scripts, analytics, or network calls. This exercise does not certify a real dataset or make an institutional decision.
