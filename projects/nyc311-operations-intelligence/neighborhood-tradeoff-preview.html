Evidence and methodology

Suggested official sources: NYC 311, NYPD Complaint Data, U.S. Census ACS, and 2020 NYC NTA boundaries. The source data must be joined to one consistent geography before import. ACS median gross rent is not a current listing price; crime reports are not a validated crime-risk score.

Further criteria to consider: commute duration, flood exposure, accessible transit, green space, air quality, housing-code violations per residential unit, and school access. Any additional variable needs documented geographic coverage and units.
