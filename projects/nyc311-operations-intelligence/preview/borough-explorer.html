Source and interpretation

Source: NYC Open Data, 311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present. Requests were grouped by borough for created_date from September 1 (inclusive) through October 1 (exclusive), 2026. The source returned 323,627 requests and 323,627 distinct request identifiers on October 16; the six reporting categories reconcile to that total.

Limits: NYC updates its dataset. These are dated aggregate observations, not a complete preserved source extract. Full-record reconciliation and the raw-file SHA-256 remain pending. No request-level records, personal addresses or neighborhood-level small cells appear here. “Unspecified” is a source category, not a sixth borough.
