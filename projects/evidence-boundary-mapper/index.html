Applied capstone

Compare two sources that describe the same outcome. Record each source separately, identify disagreements in population, measurement, timing, and causal warrant, then write a revised claim that does not exceed either source. Assess yourself on four criteria: accurate source description, provenance, explicit uncertainty, and evidence-aligned revision (0 = missing, 1 = partial, 2 = clear; 8 points total). This rubric is instructional, not validated.

Designed as a browser-local, synthetic-data learning exercise. It does not verify citations or decide whether claims are true.
