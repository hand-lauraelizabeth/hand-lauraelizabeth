# Accessibility source review

Keep source review distinct from campus accessibility ratings. For each official institution page, record the retrieval date, reviewer, institution identity, and exact feature supported. Missing statements remain UNKNOWN, not NO. Disability-services office existence is not proof of accessible buildings, housing, or transit.

Keep all institution records DO_NOT_PUBLISH until independently verified and explicitly approved.

## Separate accessibility evidence

Track disability-services contact, academic accommodations, accessible housing request process, building access, transit/path access, and digital accessibility separately. Each feature must distinguish OBSERVED_YES, OBSERVED_NO, UNKNOWN, NOT_APPLICABLE, and NOT_PUBLISHED. Never infer OBSERVED_NO from an absent page, unreported metric, or unanswered inquiry.

## QA and downstream gates

Reject duplicate/blank institution identifiers, cross-institution source mismatches, placeholder citations, undated reviews, and unsupported negative claims. Require an independently verified source for each claimed feature; preserve unverified and negative evidence as distinct states. After accessibility source review, check official financial-aid evidence (school-funded grants separately from federal/state aid), then NCES 12-code locale and source vintage, then official College Scorecard NPT41–NPT45 cohort-year mapping using Most_Recent_Inst_Cohort_Map and Institution_Cohort_Map. Record dictionary edition and cell references. ZIP release dates do not establish metric cohort years.

Only a separate independent verification and explicit owner approval can authorize release. The interactive matcher should use feature-specific evidence filters, not a composite accessibility rating. This guide is methodology, not proof of the six remaining institutions or of publication readiness.
