College + Career Matcher

Explore how affordability, campus context, housing, feature-specific accessibility evidence, and career alignment can be combined without hiding the tradeoffs behind one opaque score.

Your priorities

The five income groups follow College Scorecard reporting brackets; all example prices remain invented demonstration values, not reported net prices. The demonstration starts with synthetic records. Real institutional records are not loaded, even if a public-data.json file is present; a separate verified publication pipeline is required. Missing evidence is shown as unknown, not assumed to be a negative finding. Accessibility filters refer only to the selected feature and never imply campus-wide accessibility.

Matches

A cost ceiling requires known cost evidence. Without a ceiling, institutions with unknown costs remain visible. Rankings use confirmed points; where evidence is missing, a possible score range shows uncertainty rather than treating unknown as a negative finding.
