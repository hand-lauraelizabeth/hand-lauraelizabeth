## Functional blocks

- **B:AY** — original institutional/admissions fields, qualitative ratings, programs, deadlines, testing requirements, and demographics.
- **AZ:CL** — supplemental rankings/cost/enrollment/testing/location fields, including the later undergraduate-business additions.
- **CN:CV** — academic-profile normalization and Reach/Target/Safety admissions functions.
- **CW:DE** — numeric encodings of institution features used by preference scoring.
- **DF:DM** — eight preference contributions controlled by the user's C5:C12 settings.
- **DN:DQ** — overall fit and Reach/Target/Safety aggregate scores.
- **DR:DY** — overall ranking/tie-break/output block.
- **DZ:EG** — Reach ranking/tie-break/output block.
- **EH:EP** — Target ranking/tie-break/output block.
- **EQ:EY** — Safety ranking/tie-break/output block.

## Modernization note

Historical source/update notes such as 2016 rankings and 2017 manual updates are retained here only to document the model's lineage. The modern system should attach a current source, retrieval date, update cadence, and confidence/quality flag to each externally sourced field.
