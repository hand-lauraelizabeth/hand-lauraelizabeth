## Source and interpretation boundaries

Official Hunter publisher pages are linked in the preview. General SAT/ACT test-optional admission is **not** equivalent to nursing selection. NEX is an entrance exam; NCLEX-RN is a licensing exam. RN-to-BS is intended for applicants already holding an RN license. Shared CIP classifications and historical completions **do not** prove current offerings, seat availability, program registration, or licensure eligibility. Current admissions-cycle deadlines, prerequisites, NYSED registration and licensure must be independently checked before reliance.
