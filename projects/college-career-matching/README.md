# College + Career Explorer

[Explore the live national college search](https://www.lauraelizabethhand.com/resources/data-decision-making-tools/college-career-explorer/)

A browser-based educational search and comparison tool covering 6,243 institutions in the June 2026 U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard dataset. The interface works without an account, installation, or code execution.

## Explore and compare colleges

The live explorer supports searching by institution name, state and city; filtering by institution control, geographic setting, undergraduate size, average net price, and annual cost; and sorting by name or price. Optional SAT and ACT controls compare a student's scores against published institutional distributions when suitable data are available. Source details and reporting periods appear with the applicable figures.

**Admissions classifications are approximate comparisons, not probability predictions or admission guarantees.** Institutional test-score reporting, test-optional policies, eligibility, and individual application outcomes are distinct subjects. Missing, suppressed, and unavailable records are not silently imputed.

## Data and scope

The national index uses institution identifiers (UNITID), official federal source releases, and versioned, integrity-checked data files. Institutions, branch campuses, and certificate providers may have different reporting characteristics. Published program-completion statistics do not establish that a particular major is available for current enrollment.

Relevant public federal sources:

- [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) — institution-level data, financial-aid reporting, and admissions measures.
- [IPEDS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/DataFiles.aspx) — institutional characteristics and completions, with collection-year distinctions.
- [NCES Classification of Instructional Programs](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/cipcode/default.aspx?y=56) — standardized subject classifications.
- [O*NET Resource Center](https://www.onetcenter.org/crosswalks.html) — occupational crosswalk reference, with interpretation limitations.

## Technical implementation

The public interactive experience is progressively enhanced JavaScript with local filtering of compressed, versioned national JSON data. Tests and data validation scripts are included in the repository. Development extensions are reviewed separately from the live site; public interface features are described by what is currently supported.
