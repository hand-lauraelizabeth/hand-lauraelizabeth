# Distance data sources — Sprint 3 source research (2026-10-10)

**Scope:** Research only. No new filter, data download/check-in, school geocode, distance calculation, WordPress deployment, or claim that a campus is reachable by road. This document records source suitability for a possible later ZIP/city-origin *approximate straight-line* distance feature in the national College Search MVP.

## Decision

**Preferred inputs, subject to a separate ingest/QA task:**
1. **2026 U.S. Census Bureau Gazetteer — ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs)** for a five-character user-entered postal-code *proxy* **only when that code matches a published Census ZCTA**. The Gazetteer gives a representative **internal point**, not a USPS ZIP delivery centroid or the user's address.
2. **2026 U.S. Census Bureau Gazetteer — Places** for state-qualified incorporated places and census-designated places (CDPs). It supplies a representative **internal point**, not a school-campus address or a mathematical polygon centroid. A city's ordinary-language name does not automatically resolve to a unique Census place.
3. **USGS GNIS Populated Places / All Names** as a supplementary *name/alias research source* for settlements missing from Census Places; do not mix GNIS coordinates into automatic ranking until a separate identity and licensing/QA review.
4. **HUD–USPS ZIP Crosswalk** only as a future supplementary ZIP identity/coverage diagnostic when access and redistribution terms are confirmed; **not** a coordinate/centroid source. A USPS ZIP has no single Census-mandated geographic center.

**Do not call these source fields "centroids" in code, public copy, or documentation.** The cited fields are \`INTPTLAT\` and \`INTPTLONG\`: Census-designated internal points. This source review does *not* validate exact street addresses, commute time, road distance, school-campus location, or all currently assigned USPS ZIP codes. No distance UI is approved in this task.

## Rights, attribution, reproducibility

- **Census federal datasets:** Ordinary original U.S. federal-government works are generally in the **U.S. public domain** under 17 USC §105; this is not a blanket certification for arbitrary third-party components or uses outside the U.S. Confirm the exact archive's accompanying notices/metadata before redistributing a derived lookup. Follow Census's citation guidance: identify U.S. Census Bureau, Gazetteer 2026, specific archive/vintage, access date, and URL. Citation policy: https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html
- **USGS GNIS:** USGS says USGS-authored/produced data generally are in the U.S. public domain but warns that third-party material may differ. Consult the specific GNIS product metadata and preserve credit. Policy: https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits and https://www.usgs.gov/data-management/data-licensing
- **HUD–USPS:** HUD publicly documents use of ZIP crosswalks but access is account/token gated. **Redistribution and API terms not yet cleared** for bundling with a public client-side app. No HUD dataset is approved for direct inclusion.
- Future reproducible transform must pin archive URL, retrieval date, ZIP SHA-256, geographic vintage (2026 Gazetteer / 2020 ZCTA derivation), exact schema, row counts, unmatched counts, and derived-asset SHA-256. Never silently substitute a new version.

## Identity and precision contract for a *future* implementation

1. **Input semantics:** Accept five digits as a **string** (\`00501\` must remain \`00501\`); never store ZIP/ZCTA as an integer. The first possible match is the Census **ZCTA** \`GEOID\`, not a claim to have validated postal deliverability. An unmatched, obsolete, unique, campus, military, PO-box, or otherwise non-ZCTA USPS ZIP returns **unresolved**, not coordinates from a guessed nearby code.
2. **City semantics:** Normalize user-entered place labels for comparison without changing canonical display; require state/territory disambiguation. Join Census Places by full \`GEOID\` (state FIPS + place FIPS) once selected; do not key on bare city name, and do not silently choose among same-name places. CDPs and incorporated municipalities are not identical to postal preferred-city names.
3. **Destination semantics:** The current 6,243-school Scorecard asset identifies **school city/state** but does *not* establish campus-level lat/long. A verified, uniquely resolved Census Place internal point could support a **city-level approximation**, expressly labeled as such. If that institution's city/state cannot be uniquely resolved (e.g., ZIP-based postal city, borough/neighborhood, ambiguous alias, multiple campuses), record destination point \`null\` and **distance unavailable** until authoritative school coordinates are independently verified by UNITID.
4. **Distance semantics:** If/when two validated points exist, calculate **great-circle (straight-line) miles** via a properly checked Haversine implementation; retain unrounded values for sorting and display approximate rounded miles. It does **not** estimate driving, transit, cycling, or walking distance/time, moving costs, or accessibility. For noncontiguous regions, wrap longitude differences correctly; verify coordinate reference assumptions during ingest.
5. **Data provenance:** Store point source separately (\`census_zcta_internal_2026\`, \`census_place_internal_2026\`, possibly a later \`verified_institution_campus\`) with source vintage and identity confidence. Keep missing lat/long as **null**; do not substitute state capital, county center, nearest place, postal place name or USGS feature unless explicitly reviewed. Distinguish user-origin resolution status from school-location coverage.
6. **Coverage and fairness:** The College Scorecard snapshot includes **59 state/territory codes**, whereas Census national Gazetteers explicitly cover **50 states + DC + PR**. Expect gaps for unsupported territories and for school names/cities that do not resolve exactly. Count and display unresolved institutions before considering any filtering; do not silently exclude these from the base 6,243-directory when distance isn't selected. A future selected hard-distance cap may exclude unknown destinations but must show the exclusion count and allow Clear to recover the baseline.

**High-risk examples for subsequent tests** (fixtures/examples, not validated coordinates): \`00501\` leading zero; ZIP absent from the Census ZCTA list; noncontiguous Alaskan areas; island/overseas locations; shared city names across states (e.g., Springfield); a NYC borough or neighborhood vs New York city; a remote or branch campus whose scorecard city does not correspond to the main campus; a ZIP crossing a state line; a large rural ZCTA where the internal point differs greatly from the user's address; a military or PO-box ZIP; a private or territorial institution excluded from the Census national Gazetteer extent.
