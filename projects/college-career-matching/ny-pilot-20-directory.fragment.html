New York college pilot: 20-school source directory

Explore the actual New York schools in the initial College + Career Matcher pilot. This reference directory provides institution identifiers and routes to program, accessibility, and admissions resources while the evidence validation and live matching service are developed. It is not yet a college ranking or admissions prediction.

Research preview — not verified recommendations. The names and UNITIDs were reconciled to the June 10, 2026 College Scorecard institution extract. However, current campus locale, exact financial-aid cohorts, exhaustive programs, accessible housing availability, campus routes, and individualized admissions outcomes have not passed independent validation. Linked school pages may change. Confirm any consequential decision directly with the institution.

What has—and has not—been verified?

Verified for this reference directory: 20 unique institution identifiers matched to an official June 2026 College Scorecard extract; authoritative system, state, and college web references for further review.

Not yet certified for use in individualized matching: NCES HD2025 locale cross-checks, Scorecard field-specific aid-cohort provenance, current room inventory and vacancy, individual accessible routes, complete majors/prerequisite/transfer articulation sets, program-specific accreditation, or a validated admissions-probability model.

For CUNY, an average secondary-school grade is reported on a 100-point scale, not as a 4.0 GPA conversion, cutoff, or guarantee of admission. Special programs can impose additional requirements.

Source context: College Scorecard data (June 10, 2026 release), NCES IPEDS data, CUNY programs, and SUNY programs. Last reviewed October 9, 2026. No student-level information is collected by this directory.
