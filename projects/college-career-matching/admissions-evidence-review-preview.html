Important limits

The SAT/ACT policy applies to general freshman admission; nursing selection is a separate stage. NEX is an entrance exam, not the NCLEX-RN licensing exam. Shared CIP codes and historical completions do not establish current offerings. Program registration, current cycle deadlines, seat availability, and regulatory status require independent confirmation.
