What does this mean?
Net price is cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships—not loans. Choosing an income range uses that historical income-band average. It is not a personalized aid estimate, and this tool does not invent a family-size adjustment from data that do not support one.
Federal data support housing availability and broad residency requirements. Roommate versus private-room options require school-specific evidence and are not inferred here.
