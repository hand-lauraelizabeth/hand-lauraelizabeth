Real institutions, source-reconciled descriptive evidence. These are not ranked recommendations. Missing program, housing, transfer, or accessibility information is displayed as unknown, not treated as a negative finding. Your inputs stay in this browser.

Cost and enrollment figures reflect the June 10, 2026 College Scorecard extract; the exact underlying survey years require independent cohort verification. Income-band values are historical group averages, not personal aid estimates. Reported room prices do not establish vacancies. Admissions averages are descriptive, not cutoffs or odds. The Scorecard is not a verified inventory of all accredited schools; campus locale, program offerings, and accreditation standing require independent checks. Program and transfer rules still require source-specific verification.

These optional preferences use reviewed O*NET 31.0 Work Activity Importance ratings. They appear only when those governed attributes are loaded by the service. Missing O*NET evidence reduces coverage rather than becoming a mismatch.

What does this mean?
Net price is cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships—not loans. Choosing an income range uses that historical income-band average. It is not a personalized aid estimate, and this tool does not invent a family-size adjustment from data that do not support one.
Federal data support housing availability and broad residency requirements. Roommate versus private-room options require school-specific evidence and are not inferred here.
