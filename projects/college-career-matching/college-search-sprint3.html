        * SAFETY, TARGET and REACH are broad approximations under a conventional admissions-categorization shorthand. They describe only how a personally entered SAT or ACT score compares with 25th, 50th and 75th percentile score bands where those data have been verified. For SAT, total-score bands are estimates made by adding separately reported reading and mathematics percentile values, not officially reported composite percentiles. They are not concrete predictions or projections of admission, nor admissions probabilities. A “SAFETY” designation does not guarantee an offer. Admission policies, test optionality, academic record, application context and competition may change outcomes; the reported score bands reflect enrolled students, not all applicants.

        About this data

        This directory uses the U.S. Department of Education’s College Scorecard institution data snapshot dated June 10, 2026. It includes 6,243 institutions reported as operating in that source. It is not an independently verified list of every accredited U.S. institution.

        SAT uses the source institution-level reported average (SAT_AVG), and ACT uses the source reported composite midpoint (ACTCMMID). Score comparisons use the reported 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile section scores (SATVR25/50/75 and SATMT25/50/75), or ACT composite scores (ACTCM25/50/75). Each result card identifies its test-score source and cohort. Not every school reports scores; sliding a range excludes schools missing that metric. The optional comparison label is intentionally hidden without a personal score and verified percentile evidence. No TOEFL test-score dataset is included. School names, locations, website links, annual cost, tuition and net prices are descriptive published source figures, not quotes or admissions predictions. Tuition and fees are part of annual cost, not an additional amount. Where the source has no website address, the listing says “School website not reported.” Verify current details directly with each institution.
