Load one or more local files to see document-level corpus statistics.

Multiple local files are concatenated for analysis without inserting filenames into the analyzed text. Concordance is exact-token matching after case normalization; it is not stemming or semantic search.
Method note. Readability formulas and syllable counts are English-language heuristics. The sentiment panel is a transparent lexicon-and-negation signal, not a trained sentiment model and not a judgment about meaning, tone, quality, or intent. Frequency, collocation, concordance, and similarity measures describe surface lexical patterns; interpretation remains with the reader. PMI can over-emphasize rare pairs, so Text Lens requires a user-selected minimum occurrence count.
