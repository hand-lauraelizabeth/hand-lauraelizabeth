Concordance & corpus tools

Local analysis

Search a word or phrase in context, adjust the context window, and inspect collocations that recur more often than their component-word frequencies would suggest.

Manage loaded documents

Reordering changes display/export order within Text A or Text B; removing a document rebuilds that text from the remaining loaded files.

Enter a term or phrase to see up to 50 concordance lines.

Load one or more local files to see document-level corpus statistics.

Multiple local files are concatenated for whole-text analysis without inserting filenames into the analyzed text, while document identity is retained for corpus comparison. You can reorder/remove loaded documents, focus one document, or select an explicit keyness reference. Concordance is exact-token matching after case normalization; it is not stemming or semantic search.
Method note. Readability formulas and syllable counts are English-language heuristics. The sentiment panel is a transparent lexicon-and-negation signal, not a trained sentiment model and not a judgment about meaning, tone, quality, or intent. Frequency, collocation, concordance, and similarity measures describe surface lexical patterns; interpretation remains with the reader. PMI can over-emphasize rare pairs, so Text Lens requires a user-selected minimum occurrence count. Cross-document distinctive terms use signed log-likelihood G² against the remainder of the loaded corpus with a separate minimum-frequency floor; lexical similarity is not semantic similarity.
