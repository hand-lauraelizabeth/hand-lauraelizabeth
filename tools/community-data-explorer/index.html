Community Data Explorer

Compare locally imported American Community Survey estimates with explicit source identifiers and period checks. This tool does not retrieve, verify, or transmit data.

Before interpreting: ACS estimates have sampling uncertainty. This tool does not establish statistical significance, adjust for inflation, or resolve changing geographic definitions.

1. Import a CSV

UTF-8 CSV, up to 2,000,000 bytes and 5,000 records. Exact columns, in order: source_id,geography,variable,series,period_start,period_end,estimate,moe. Series is acs1 or acs5. Missing estimate/MOE: empty field or explicit Census missing code. No personal information needed. Importing another valid file will ask before replacing your current local records; cancelled or invalid imports keep the current evidence and export.
